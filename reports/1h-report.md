### Elder Gond Devotional
There are a number of things that spoke to me as the core of Elder Gong's message. I appreciated his reminder that AI cannot become or replace God, no matter how advanced it gets. There's a lot of fear in today's world regarding the potential capabilities of AI and how it could even surpass humans in almost all areas. It will become more important than ever to remember these truths and decide for ourselves: how do we value humans? Hopefully, the answer to that question is one that emphasizes our relationships, agency, and power to do righteous work to spur the growth of others, and ourselves. We should not devalue human value.

I've decided I never want to allow AI to replace human social interaction. I also don't want to only rely on AI when it comes to making decisions that have moral consequences, or impact other people's agency in any way. I know that's a broad range of things, and I think it's a very case by case decision. But overall humans are necessary and must way in that have those kinds of consequences.

### Additional Exploration
I spent some additional time researching the built-in tools that Anthropic gives their claude models. I was also curious if there was any potential impact of a model too many tools, and if that impacts performance. The main impacts were:
- context bloat
- selection accuracy dropping
- attention dilution (potentially causing it to ignore system instructions)
- hallucinations

As models get better it may be necessary to re-examine what tools are beneficial to give models.
