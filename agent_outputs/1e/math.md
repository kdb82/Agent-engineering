# Math Solutions

**User:** A shirt costs $40 and is discounted by 25%. The discounted price is then subject to 8% sales tax. What is the final price?

**Agent:** The discounted price is:

$$
40 \times (1 - 0.25) = 40 \times 0.75 = \$30
$$

Adding 8% sales tax:

$$
30 \times 1.08 = \$32.40
$$

**Final price: \$32.40**

**User:** Solve for all real values of $x$: $2x^2 - 7x - 15 = 0$

**Agent:** Factor the quadratic:

$$
2x^2-7x-15=(2x+3)(x-5)
$$

Set each factor equal to zero:

$$
2x+3=0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x=-\frac{3}{2}
$$

$$
x-5=0 \quad
$$

USER: A fair six-sided die is rolled repeatedly until a 6 appears. What is the expected number of rolls?

AGENT: Each roll has a probability of \(\frac{1}{6}\) of being a 6. The expected number of rolls until the first success is:

$$
\frac{1}{p}=\frac{1}{1/6}=6
$$

$$
\boxed{6\text{ rolls}}
$$
