USER: what was the latest talk from bednar about?

AGENT: Elder David A. Bednar’s latest talk, **“All Who Have Endured Valiantly,”** was about the spiritual meaning of **enduring to the end**. He taught that it is more than merely persevering through difficulties; it is a lifelong process of becoming spiritually transformed and more like Jesus Christ through His grace. A central focus was the spiritual gift of **charity—the pure love of Christ**—and how receiving that gift changes our character.

> Enduring to the end is the joyous quest of a lifetime—a pressing forward with faith in Jesus Christ in a gradual process of trusting in and receiving help from our Savior to become more like Him.

**David A. Bednar**, “All Who Have Endured Valiantly”

AGENT: Yes. Here are several other relevant passages from Elder Bednar’s talk:

> The Savior’s restored gospel invites us to be spiritually transformed—not merely to improve our behavior. As we align our character, desires, actions, and what we truly love more closely with God’s will, the Savior can bring about a comprehensive and complete change in us.

> We are to become new creatures in Christ.

> As we follow, love, and serve the Savior, we gradually focus less on our own desires and interests and more on understanding and addressing the needs of others. We do not merely perform benevolent deeds; rather, our state of being is changed and becomes increasingly Christlike. Charity, then, ultimately possesses us.

> Enduring to the end is not merely a relentless determination to grit our teeth, hold on to the limits of our physical strength and mental capacity, and push through the challenges and adversities of mortal life; it is so much more than that.

> The scriptural phrase “endure to the end” is a repeated reminder of the mighty spiritual change in our hearts that should be ongoing throughout our lives; it also is His promise of what we may become if we truly are possessed of the pure love of Christ.

**David A. Bednar**, “All Who Have Endured Valiantly”
