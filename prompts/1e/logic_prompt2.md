The Puzzle

Six houses stand in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. Each house has one resident, one pet, one favorite drink and one paint color, and no two houses share any of them.

People: Ava, Ben, Cara, Dev, Eli, Faye
Pets: bird, cat, dog, fish, rabbit, turtle
Drinks: coffee, juice, lemonade, milk, tea, water
House colors: black, blue, green, red, white, yellow

How to read the clues:

"Immediately to the left" means the house number is exactly one lower.
"Next door" means the house numbers differ by exactly one.
"Exactly one is true" means one statement is true and the other is false.

Clues:

Exactly one is true: (a) Cara lives next door to Faye. (b) The milk drinker lives in the green house.
The coffee drinker lives somewhere to the left of the blue house.
Exactly one is true: (a) Dev lives immediately to the left of Ava. (b) Eli lives next door to the bird owner.
Eli owns either the rabbit or the fish.
Exactly one is true: (a) The red house is immediately to the left of Ben's house. (b) Dev lives in the blue house.
Exactly one is true: (a) The red house is immediately to the left of the rabbit owner's house. (b) The white house is immediately to the left of the green house.
Exactly one is true: (a) The juice drinker lives next door to the fish owner. (b) The blue house is one of the two end houses.
Exactly one is true: (a) The milk drinker lives in one of the two end houses. (b) The water drinker lives next door to Cara.
The rabbit owner lives in either the green house or the black house.
The blue house is somewhere to the left of the yellow house.
The tea drinker is either Ava or Cara.
The bird owner lives immediately to the left of Dev.
Exactly one is true: (a) Ava lives in one of the two end houses. (b) The milk drinker lives in one of the two end houses.
There are exactly two houses between the turtle owner and Eli.
Exactly one is true: (a) The rabbit owner lives immediately to the left of the yellow house. (b) Cara drinks coffee.
The cat owner lives in an odd-numbered house.
There are exactly two houses between Eli and the coffee drinker.
The tea drinker lives in either the white house or the green house.
Ava owns either the dog or the cat.

Question: Give the full solution: who lives in each house, and what pet, drink and house color goes with each house? Explain your reasoning step by step, and explain which statement is true in each "exactly one" clue.
